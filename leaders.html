Preparation Guide

OneAccord gatherings succeed through humble, prepared leadership. This page is for pastors, worship leaders, and musicians.

⛪ For Pastors

Preach

20 minutes. Christ and the Holy Spirit's power. Come with a clear word — then release the room to God.

Participate

After preaching, step back. Let others testify, ask questions, and share. Facilitate — don't dominate.

Order

1 Corinthians 14:40 — "Let all things be done decently and in order." Written questions, one speaker at a time.

Humility

Come as a servant. Your people are watching. Seated pastors who worship create a culture of expectation.

🎵 For Musicians & Worship Leaders

One Leader

One chosen worship leader per gathering. All other musicians follow that leader — not each other, not their preferences.

One Sound

"When the trumpeters and singers were as one…" (2 Chronicles 5:13). All instruments unified under God's glory.

All Welcome

Winds, strings, percussion, keys — bring your instrument. Psalm 150 calls everything with breath and string to praise.

Volume

Play at a level that invites the congregation to sing and participate — not so loud that their voices are drowned out.
